07 · Questions

Good to know

How much does a project cost?

Every project is different, so we give a clear quote after your brief. Social media handling and management come as monthly plans. The quote is free.

Do you also shoot, or only edit?

Both. We can come with camera, lights and sound to shoot your content, or edit footage you send us.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

All kinds: shops, restaurants, clinics, real estate, fashion, coaches, creators, events and more. Every client gets the same care.

How fast is delivery?

Reels and thumbnails are usually ready in a few days; bigger shoots and websites take longer. We confirm the exact date in the quote.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. Briefs, drafts and payments all happen online, so we work with clients anywhere in the world.

Can I edit my website myself later?

Yes. Our websites can be edited without coding, and we show you how.

Do you make mobile apps and computer software?

Yes. We build Android and iOS apps, Windows / Mac software, and connected combo apps that work on PC and phone together (for schools, shops, clinics and offices), exactly to your requirement, and can publish your app on Play Store and App Store.
