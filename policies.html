Privacy policy

Flow Studio ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Your name, business name, Gmail address, mobile / WhatsApp number and (optionally) address when you sign up or ask for a quotation.
  • Files you upload for your project (logos, photos, videos, documents) and the messages, voice notes and ratings you send in the dashboard.
  • Payment status (we do not store card, UPI PIN or bank login details — payments are handled by the payment provider).
  • Basic sign-in records (time, device and city) to keep accounts safe.

How we use it

  • To prepare quotations, do the work, deliver files and send updates.
  • To send invoices and payment receipts.
  • To improve our service and keep the dashboard secure.

Sharing

We never sell your data. We share it only with services needed to run Flow Studio (Google sign-in and storage, our hosting and our payment provider), or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can update your details in the dashboard at any time, or write to us to delete your account and files. Samples of finished work may be shown in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

Terms of service

  • Every project starts with a written quotation in your dashboard. The quotation lists the work, the price and the timeline.
  • You confirm that you own, or have permission to use, every file, logo, music and brand material you send us.
  • Draft previews are shared in low quality with a Flow Studio watermark. Full-quality final files are released after full payment.
  • Each quotation includes the number of revisions. Extra changes or new work outside the quotation are quoted separately.
  • Once fully paid, you may use the final files for your business. Flow Studio may show the work in its portfolio unless you ask us not to.
  • AI tools may be used to create parts of the work; AI-made content is labelled where the platforms require it.
  • We may refuse work that is illegal, hateful, misleading or harmful.

Payments

40% advance to start the work · 60% balance before final delivery. If the advance was not paid, the full amount is due before delivery.

Payments are accepted by UPI, bank transfer and our online payment link (cards, UPI, net banking; international clients can pay in USD). Every payment is confirmed in your dashboard and a bill (invoice) is issued.

Refund & cancellation

  • Before work starts: you can cancel and get a full refund of the advance.
  • After work has started: the advance covers the work already done and is not refundable. If you cancel, we deliver what was completed up to that point.
  • Our mistake: if we cannot deliver what was agreed in the quotation, we refund the amount for the part not delivered.
  • After final delivery and full payment: no refund, because the files have been delivered — but we will fix any genuine error in our work free of charge.
  • Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.

Delivery

All our services are digital. Drafts and final files are delivered in your Flow Studio dashboard (and by Google Drive link for very large files) — nothing is shipped by post unless a printed product is part of your quotation, in which case the delivery time and charges are written in the quotation. Timelines start after the advance and your files are received.
